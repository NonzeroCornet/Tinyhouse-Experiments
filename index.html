Rules

K King: one step any way. W Wazir: one step straight. F Ferz: one step diagonally. H Horse: like a knight, but blocked by a piece on the square straight next to it.

P Pawn: one step forward, captures diagonally, promotes to F, W or H. Promoted pieces (pink + badge) turn back into pawns when captured.

Captured pieces go to your hand; click one, then an empty square, to drop it. Pawns can be dropped anywhere except the far rank.

Checkmate wins. A stalemated player wins. Threefold repetition or 200 plies is a draw.
